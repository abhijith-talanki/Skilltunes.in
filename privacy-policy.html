Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Notice for Skilltunes OPC Private Limited, a one person company incorporated in India ("Skilltunes", "we", "us", or "our") describes how and why we may access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

  • Download and use our mobile application, ModuleMind, or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice
  • Use ModuleMind — an app for SAP consultants preparing for interviews: a platform where users practice SAP questions through quizzes, practice interview questions, and AI mock interviews, with scores and a performance dashboard
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including any marketing or events

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. We are responsible for making decisions about how your personal information is processed. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have questions or concerns, contact us at modulemindsap@gmail.com.

Summary of Key Points

What personal information do we process? When you use our Services, we may process personal information depending on how you interact with us, the choices you make, and the features you use. See What Information Do We Collect? below.

Do we process any sensitive personal information? No. We do not currently process any special or sensitive categories of personal information (such as biometric, health, racial/ethnic, or genetic data).

Do we collect any information from third parties? Yes, in a limited way — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. See How Do We Handle Your Social Logins? below.

How do we process your information? We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law. See How Do We Process Your Information? below.

In what situations and with which parties do we share personal information? We share information with specific service providers we rely on to operate the Services, and in specific situations described below. See When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

How do we keep your information safe? We use reasonable organizational and technical safeguards, but no system is 100% secure. See How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

What are your rights? Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. See What Are Your Privacy Rights?

How do you exercise your rights? Email us at modulemindsap@gmail.com or submit a data subject access request.

7. Do We Offer Artificial Intelligence-Based Products?

In Short: Yes — AI-powered features for mock interview scoring.

We provide AI-powered features through a third-party AI service provider, Anthropic. All personal information processed using our AI features is handled in line with this Privacy Notice and our agreement with Anthropic.

18. Geolocation Information

We request access to a coarse, approximate location from your device solely to determine your country (e.g., India vs. other regions) so we can show correct local pricing and currency. We do not continuously track your location, and we do not store precise GPS coordinates on our servers.

19. Reminder Notifications

If you enable them, we schedule daily study-streak reminder notifications locally on your device. These are not sent from our servers — we do not operate server-side push notifications, and no push token is transmitted to us.

20. Account Deletion

If you request deletion of your account, we permanently delete or anonymize your personal information within 7 days of a verified request.

21. Mobile Device Access — Storage, Microphone & Voice Recordings

We request storage/photo-library access only when you choose to upload a profile photo. We do not access your device storage at any other time.

We request microphone access for two features:

  • AI mock interview practice: your spoken answers are converted to text entirely on your device. Only the resulting text transcript is sent to our servers and to our AI provider (Anthropic) for scoring — no audio recording is created or transmitted for this feature.
  • Self-guided practice mode: if you choose to record an answer for your own playback and review, that recording is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers or any third party. You can delete it at any time from the practice screen, and it will not transfer if you switch devices.
