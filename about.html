About

An independent company
focused on SAP skills.

Skilltunes OPC Private Limited was incorporated in Bengaluru to build practical learning tools for SAP consultants — starting with finance and controlling, where the gap between knowing the theory and handling a real interview or ticket is widest.

What we build

ModuleMind, an Android app for SAP FICO interview and certification practice: curated question banks with STAR-format answers, AI mock interviews, quizzes, T-code and table references and a mastery-tracking learning roadmap. Further SAP modules are planned on the same platform.

Who it is for

Working consultants preparing for their next role, support analysts moving into functional roles, and candidates preparing for SAP certification who want to practise speaking answers, not just reading them.

Our principles

Content written by practitioners and reviewed against real configuration. Clear pricing with a free trial. Minimal data collection, explained plainly in our Privacy Policy.
